Teacher introduction · about 3 minutes

1. How does a computer work?

Every computer follows the same basic idea. We give it information, it works on that information, and it shows us a result.

⌨️ 🖱️INPUT

We give the computer instructions or data.

Keyboard, mouse, microphone
🧠 ⚙️PROCESS

The CPU follows instructions and works with the data.

CPU + RAM help run programs
🖥️ 🔊OUTPUT

The computer shows or plays the result.

Monitor, speakers, printer
INPUTPROCESSOUTPUT
Class example: You press W in a game (input) → the CPU processes the movement and the GPU creates the next image (process) → your character moves on the monitor (output). Storage saves your game and files for later.
Hardware basics · about 5 minutes

2. Meet the parts inside a PC

Know these names before you choose them on BuildCores. The moving fans and glowing PC are simple animations to help you recognise parts.

CPU
Processing

CPU · Processor

The computer’s main brain. Runs instructions, apps and games.

Graphics

GPU · Graphics

Creates game and video images. Powerful GPUs help demanding games and editing.

Temporary memory

RAM · Memory

Short-term workspace while programs run. More RAM helps with multiple tasks.

Long-term storage

SSD / HDD

Stores games, apps, photos and videos. SSDs are usually much faster than HDDs.

Connections

Motherboard

The main circuit board that connects the parts. Must match the CPU and RAM.

✣
Temperature

CPU Cooler + Fans

Removes heat. Air and liquid cooling help stop overheating.

⚡
Electricity

Power Supply (PSU)

Supplies electricity to the parts. It needs enough watts for the system.

Size + appearance

Case

Holds and protects the parts. Check size, airflow, colour and fit.

Two things to remember: RAM is temporary memory; an SSD or HDD saves files even when the PC is off. Some CPUs have integrated graphics, so a separate graphics card is not always needed. Wi-Fi may be built into a motherboard or provided by an adapter.

BuildCores · Step 5

4. Build your simulated PC step by step

Keep your planning sheet in one window and BuildCores in another. Use the steps in this order, matching page 7 of your task sheet.

1
Open au.buildcores.com ↗

Click Start Building → Start from scratch. You can start without an account. Handle any cookie notice as appropriate.

2
Choose your CASE first

Select a case that fits your client: white for Jayden, room for Priya’s water cooling, or small for the Wilsons. Use search or filters, then Add to build.

3
Add the main parts

Use Add Part to select CPU → Motherboard → GPU (if needed) → RAM → CPU Cooler → Storage → Power Supply. Add extra storage and case fans where needed.

4
Check that parts fit together

Keep the Compatibility Filter turned on where available. Check that the CPU fits the motherboard, RAM fits, the graphics card and cooler fit the case, and cooling is suitable. Avoid parts with N/A Australian prices.

5
Watch the top bar

Check the A$ total, Compatible status and estimated power usage in watts. Stay under your client’s budget. Follow your planning sheet’s guide of a PSU with plenty of spare capacity (approximately double the displayed wattage).

6
Name and inspect your build

Use the pencil next to New Build, name it with your name and client, then Save Changes. Rotate the 3D view to inspect the PC. If a button differs, look for its current equivalent.

7
Record everything in the planning sheet

Copy the exact model and A$ price of every part. Write the final total. Complete all checks on page 8. Use Share → Link and paste the link into the sheet.

8
Take a screenshot and hand in

Show your PC clearly in 3D. Press Windows + Shift + S, drag over the PC and paste the screenshot into your sheet. Save your document and submit it on Compass.
